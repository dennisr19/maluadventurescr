# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro 7, React islands, shadcn/ui with Base UI primitives, Tailwind CSS 4, Tabler Icons,
and static deployment to Cloudflare Workers.

## Users

Travelers comparing activities in Costa Rica and looking for a direct way to contact the
operator. The page supports English and Spanish through dedicated static routes.

## Product Purpose

Present Malu Adventures and make its available tours, price options, and contact information
easy to browse. Success is a visitor finding a suitable experience and starting a WhatsApp
conversation with the operator.

## Operating Context

Visitors may browse on mobile while traveling. The catalog must support text search and
category filtering without requiring an account or checkout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Searchable, filterable tour catalog.
- Direct WhatsApp and email contact.
- Cash prices and the supplied 13% card tax notice.
- Static-first architecture suitable for Cloudflare.
- Online booking, availability, durations, and meeting points are open decisions and must not
  be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Malu Adventures.
- Tagline: Discover. Explore. Live the adventures.
- A clean white visual foundation with coordinated ocean blue and jungle green accents.
- An expressive hero title, consistent content width, clear CTA hierarchy, and future room for
  bird SVG illustrations.

## Evidence on Hand

- The supplied price-list image is the authority for tour names, prices, phone, email, and
  payment note.
- Hero photography: Manuel Antonio Beach by Jake Marsee on Pexels, stored at
  `public/images/manuel-antonio-hero.jpg`.
- Tour departure times were supplied separately and are recorded in the catalog data.
- No testimonials, ratings, durations, inclusions, availability, or booking terms were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Let the destination lead and keep the interface calm.
- Make comparison fast without hiding price conditions.
- Keep one obvious primary action: contact the operator on WhatsApp.
- Preserve factual accuracy and expose unknowns honestly.
